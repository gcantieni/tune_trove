This collection contains the tunes in Ryan's Mammoth Collection of Fiddle Tunes and the later republished in an edition by Cole: 1000 Fiddle Tunes.

These are the same book. Ryan's was the original edition, published in 1884 in Boston  Massachusetts. In 1940, the Cole Publishing Company republished it without giving credit to the original editor or publisher.
